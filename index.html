Questions

Will it make my videos look worse?

You pick how they should look; at the default level the difference is engineered to be imperceptible, and the app scores its output against the original to hold that promise.

Does it upload my videos?

No. Everything happens on your machine.

What if it can't make a file smaller?

It tells you and skips — you never end up with a bigger file.

Which platforms?

Windows 10/11 (64-bit), macOS 13+ on Apple Silicon (signed and notarized), and Linux (AppImage, .deb; Flathub listing in submission).

Why AV1?

Same look, roughly half the size of H.264. A one-switch "More compatible" (HEVC) mode exists for older devices.

Refunds and lost keys?

Both handled via Lemon Squeezy — recover a key with your purchase email; refunds deactivate the license automatically.
