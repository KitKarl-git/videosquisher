You pick the quality, it does the rest

A word, not a number

Smallest, Reduced, Good enough, Unnoticeable, Original. If you don't know what CRF means, good. You don't need to.

It grades its own homework

Each quality level is test encoded and scored against your original with perceptual metrics, the same kind streaming companies use to check their streams.

The smallest file that passes

It works per video: compress as hard as possible while still meeting the quality you asked for.

Never hands you a bigger file

If the result isn't smaller, it's thrown away. If there's no real saving to be had (under 5%), it tells you instead of wasting a re-encode.

Need it under 25 MB?

Type the size you need and it finds the best quality that fits. Handy for email attachments and upload limits.

New codecs, and it's fast

AV1 by default, HEVC with one switch for older devices. Uses NVIDIA, Intel, AMD or Apple hardware encoding when it's there. FFmpeg comes inside, nothing else to install.

Questions

Will my videos look worse?

You decide how they should look. At the default level the app scores its own output against the original to hold that line, and it won't keep a file that doesn't pass.

Does it upload my videos anywhere?

No. Everything happens on your machine.

What if a file can't get smaller?

It tells you and moves on. You never end up with a file bigger than the one you started with.

Which platforms?

Windows 10/11 (64-bit), macOS 13+ on Apple Silicon (signed and notarized), and Linux (AppImage or .deb).

Why AV1?

Same look, roughly half the size of H.264. If older devices need to play the file, flip the "more compatible" switch and it uses HEVC instead.

Lost your key, or want a refund?

Both go through Lemon Squeezy. Your purchase email recovers your key; a refund deactivates the license automatically.
